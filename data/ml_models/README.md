# CyberGuard Spectra Series

A modular suite for next-generation cyber defense, powered by knowledge graphs and GNNs.

## Modules

### Spectra Sentry
- Intrusion prevention, detection, and continuous monitoring
- Real-time analytics, alerting, and risk scoring

### Spectra Guardian
- Intrusion response and mitigation
- Automated and guided response orchestration

### Spectra Phoenix
- Intrusion recovery and self-healing
- Automated patching, rollback, and reconfiguration

---

All modules leverage a unified knowledge graph and custom GNN models for advanced analytics and automation.
